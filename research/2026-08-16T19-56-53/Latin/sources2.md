## Latest Releases in Latin Music - August 2023

1. **Maluma** released his highly anticipated new album *Don Juan* on August 25, introducing a new alter ego and featuring the single "Trofeo" with Yandel, which features Leo Messi. The album signals a new era for the artist, accentuating his international stardom.

2. **Karol G's** latest album *Tropicoqueta* showcases her 2023 evolution as an artist, blending reggaeton with various Latin styles, using live instruments more than before. This album serves to amplify her market reach, aiming for a global audience while also reflecting her roots.

3. **Feid** and **Ryan Castro** reunited for the track "Mentira" released on August 6, continuing their vibrant collaboration history, and adding excitement back into the reggaeton catalogue. Additional releases include tracks from **Luis Fonsi**, **Ozuna**, and **Alejandro Sanz**.

## Notable Artists in Latin Genre - August 2023

- **Karol G** made headlines by earning the most entries on the Billboard Hot Latin Songs chart, with her track "Mi Ex Tenía Razón" reaching No. 1.
- **Ozuna** continues to expand his influence in the genre, celebrating multiple records and maintaining a robust music video presence.

## Trends in Latin Music - 2023

- The global expansion of *música mexicana* solidifies its presence on international charts, as hybrid genres blend traditional sounds with modern urban music.
- There were significant increases in the popularity of *bachata* and regional music movements, marking a shift in mainstream preferences and expanding audience engagement.
- The demand for live music events surged by 40% compared to the previous year, indicating a strong recovery and a longing for interpersonal artist-fan experiences.
